Hon. Betty Amongi Meets Women Leaders in Lira

12 January 2026  ·  Foundation Communications Team  ·  4 Comments

Hundreds of women from across the Lango sub-region gathered in Lira City this week for a community forum on economic empowerment and leadership, hosted in partnership with local district leaders. The event, which drew participants from Lira, Alebtong, Dokolo, and Otuke, focused on practical pathways for women to access funding, mentorship, and markets for their businesses.

Speaking to a crowd wrapped in vibrant traditional dress, the Foundation's founder underscored a message that has anchored the organization's work since its inception: that community transformation starts with the people closest to the ground.

"When we invest in a woman, we are investing in a household, a market stall, a classroom, and a whole community that follows her lead. That is why this Foundation exists."

The forum featured breakout sessions on savings groups, agribusiness value chains, and digital literacy, facilitated by Foundation staff alongside representatives from partner organizations including The Unlimited Youths. Several attendees who had previously received seed funding through the Foundation's Women & Girls program shared their experiences, encouraging others to apply for the next funding cycle.

The Foundation plans to hold similar forums in Gulu and Kampala later this year as part of its continued push to reach women entrepreneurs across Northern Uganda. Applications for the next round of seed funding open in the coming weeks — interested applicants are encouraged to follow the Foundation's pages for updates.
