About Us

Empowering Youth & Communities

The Betty Amongi Foundation is committed to transforming lives in Northern Uganda, especially within the Lango sub-region.

Who We Are

Building a Generation Ready to Lead

We support young people, entrepreneurs, and innovators by providing seed capital, mentorship, training, and opportunities to connect with global partners. Our work is centered on building a generation that is creative, business-oriented, and ready to drive sustainable development in their communities.

Our Mission

To equip youth, women, and entrepreneurs across the Lango sub-region with seed funding, mentorship, and skills development that drive sustainable community development.

Our Vision

A Northern Uganda where every young person, woman, and local entrepreneur has access to the opportunity and support they need to thrive.
