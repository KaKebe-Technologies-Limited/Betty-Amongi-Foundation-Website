What is MentorMe 360?

Building Lango’s Next Generation of Leaders

MentorMe 360 is a three-month intensive mentorship initiative designed to build a pipeline of 100 young professionals from the Lango sub-region. Running from September to November 2026, the program combines physical sessions in Lira, Gulu, and Kampala with twice-monthly virtual sessions — giving participants hands-on training, mentorship, and a national network of changemakers.

The name “360” reflects our commitment to whole-person development — a full 360-degree approach to leadership, ICT & innovation, personal growth, and entrepreneurship. Every participant graduates with a personal development plan, a completed portfolio of assessments, and direct mentorship from industry leaders.

100% Free for Participants. MentorMe 360 is fully funded by the Betty Amongi Foundation and implemented on the ground by The Unlimited Youths — there is no fee to apply or to participate.

The Four Pillars

A Well-Rounded Growth Journey

Every participant is trained across four interconnected pillars designed to build complete, career-ready young professionals.

Leadership

Communication, decision-making, and community leadership skills for the next generation of change agents.

ICT & Innovation

Digital literacy, emerging technology, and innovation methods to solve local problems with global tools.

Personal Growth

Emotional intelligence, goal setting, and wellbeing practices that build resilient, self-aware individuals.

Entrepreneurship

Business planning, financial literacy, and venture-building skills to create sustainable livelihoods.
