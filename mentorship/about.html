About The Program

Why MentorMe 360 Exists

A response to a real gap: talented young people in the Lango sub-region with limited access to structured mentorship, professional networks, and future-ready skills.

Background & Rationale

Closing the Mentorship Gap in Northern Uganda

Youth unemployment and underemployment remain critical challenges across the Lango sub-region. While many young people leave school or university with academic qualifications, few have access to the leadership exposure, digital skills, entrepreneurial guidance, and personal development support needed to translate potential into impact.

MentorMe 360 was designed to directly address this gap — pairing 100 young professionals with experienced mentors across four critical pillars, delivered through a hybrid model that respects both the realities of rural access and the opportunities of digital connectivity.

The program is fully funded by the Betty Amongi Foundation and implemented on the ground by The Unlimited Youths, which is why cost is never a barrier to a young person's participation.

Our Mission

To equip 100 young professionals from the Lango sub-region each cohort with the leadership, technical, entrepreneurial, and personal growth skills required to drive sustainable community development.

Our Vision

A Lango sub-region where every young person has access to mentorship, opportunity, and the tools to become a leader in their community and beyond.

A fully-funded program. Every cost of MentorMe 360 — training, materials, facilitation, and the graduation ceremony — is covered by the Betty Amongi Foundation. Participants selected into the program pay nothing.
Program Pillars

Four Pillars, One Complete Professional

Leadership

Public speaking, team management, ethical decision-making, and community organizing.

ICT & Innovation

Digital tools, data literacy, design thinking, and applied technology for local problems.

Personal Growth

Self-awareness, emotional intelligence, time management, and wellbeing.

Entrepreneurship

Business modelling, financial literacy, pitching, and access to seed opportunities.
