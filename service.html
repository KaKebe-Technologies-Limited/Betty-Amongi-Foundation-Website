Driving Impact Through Innovation & Empowerment

The Betty Amongi Foundation supports youth, women, and entrepreneurs with programs designed to spark creativity, strengthen communities, and open doors to global opportunities.

A fully-funded, three-month mentorship program for 100 young professionals from the Lango sub-region — leadership, ICT & innovation, personal growth, and entrepreneurship.
